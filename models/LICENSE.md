DermaMNIST-C image data and the included low-resolution demo images are licensed
CC BY-NC 4.0 by the original dataset authors. Corrected metadata is CC BY 4.0.
The original source images were resized to 28×28 by the corrected release's
authors; included PNG demo images are exports of those arrays, with original
ISIC IDs retained. No clinical meaning has been added to those images.
