# Educational content sources

Sources checked October 11, 2026. App copy is concise original paraphrase; the sources' photographs and full articles are not redistributed. These references support general education, not the classifier's performance or an individual diagnosis.

| Content | Source and purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Changing, growing, bleeding or nonhealing spots; appearance cannot establish diagnosis | [American Academy of Dermatology: Skin cancer](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/skin-cancer) |
| Rosacea and overlapping signs, including variation across skin tones | [AAD: What is rosacea?](https://www.aad.org/public/diseases/rosacea/what-is) |
| Basic cleanser, moisturizer and sun protection routine | [AAD: Skin care on a budget](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/skin-care-budget) |
| Gentle cleansing and avoiding abrasive scrubbing | [AAD: Face washing 101](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/skin-care-basics/care/face-washing-101) |
| Broad spectrum, SPF 30+ and water resistance; reapplication per product directions | [AAD: Choosing sunscreen](https://www.aad.org/public/everyday-care/sun-protection/shade-clothing-sunscreen/choosing-right-sunscreen) |

No prescription medicine is recommended from an uploaded image. No product availability, price, rating, affiliate relationship or purchase is claimed. Ingredient explanations are general mechanisms, not personalized treatments. Persistent symptoms and changing/concerning lesions warrant professional assessment regardless of the app output. Content should undergo clinical/editorial review before any patient-facing clinical service.
